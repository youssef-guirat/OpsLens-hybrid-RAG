# Incident Severity Policy

## P1 — Critical

P1 applies to a complete purchase outage, confirmed or suspected duplicate charges, material security exposure, unrecoverable data loss, or impact to more than 25 percent of active customers. Page the incident commander and owning team immediately. Create a public-facing update within fifteen minutes when customers are affected. Updates continue at least every thirty minutes.

## P2 — Major

P2 applies to severe degradation of a primary workflow, regional unavailability, or sustained impact to 5–25 percent of active customers. Assign an incident commander and technical lead. Internal updates occur every thirty minutes, and Customer Support receives a summary.

## P3 — Minor

P3 applies to limited degradation with a workaround, impact below 5 percent, or a non-critical internal service failure. The owning team coordinates response during the current on-call period. A dedicated incident commander is optional.

## P4 — Informational

P4 is used for low-risk defects, monitoring gaps, and near misses without current customer impact. Track the work in the owning team's backlog.

## Classification rules

Use the highest plausible severity while facts are uncertain, then downgrade with evidence. Potential financial duplication and credential exposure remain P1 until disproved. Severity measures impact and urgency, not how difficult the repair appears.
